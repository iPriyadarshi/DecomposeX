Why this calculator uses fractions

Ordinary computer arithmetic cannot represent most decimals. The classic demonstration is that a tenth plus a fifth is not three tenths: 

> 0.1 + 0.2 === 0.3
false
> 0.1 + 0.2
0.30000000000000004

In a factorization those errors compound. A pivot that is mathematically zero comes out as 4.9e-17, so a test for “is this pivot zero?” silently fails, and the algorithm divides by near-nothing and returns garbage dressed up to two decimal places.

DecomposeX stores every number as an exact ratio of arbitrary-precision integers. A zero pivot is exactly zero and gets handled properly, and 1/3 stays 1/3.
